## Product Identity (CURRENT)
Aura is an AI Professional Identity Platform. It continuously turns a senior professional's existing knowledge consumption into compounding market presence, without adding work to their week.

Headline: "Your experience is worth more than your profile shows."

Audience: senior professionals with hard-won expertise across the GCC and wider Arab world, ANY industry. Register is senior-executive. Status and exclusivity matter.

RETIRED — treat as a bug if seen anywhere: "Your expertise is invisible. Aura fixes that." · "Strategic Intelligence OS" · any framing that restricts Aura to water, utilities, power or infrastructure · any EY-specific framing · "Zone of Genius" · personal-branding-coach language.

## Banned words — never generate these in code, copy, comments or UI strings
authority (as a noun) · trajectory · personal brand · thought leader · thought leadership · leverage (as a verb) · utilize · facilitate · seamless · regenerate (member-facing) · Zone of Genius.
Replacements: authority -> presence or standing · leverage/utilize -> use · thought leadership -> sharing your expertise · regenerate -> "Hear me again".

## Design law — System-B "Signal" (CURRENT, LIVE)
Authority order: AURA_DESIGN_LAW.md -> Aura_Design_Foundations_System-B.md -> aura-tokens-system-b.css. Anything older is history, not instruction.

## Content rules (LinkedIn output)
Mobile-first: 1-2 sentences per paragraph. Hook in the first 1-2 lines. Concept -> short explanation -> example. Numbers, experiments and real results carry the weight. One closing action, never a menu of options.

Every claim must trace to the member's own captured evidence. Never invent a statistic. Never claim what any external firm, competitor or "the market" has published — Aura cannot see the open web.

Nothing is hardcoded per user. Every output is generated from that member's assessments, captures and voice training.

## Arabic standards
Contemporary professional Arabic — not dialect, not bureaucratic MSA. Keep English technical terms in English. Short lines, tension before insight, one specific number mid-post, closing uncomfortable question. Formatting: ◆ for main points, ↳ for sub-points but NEVER ↳ in RTL Arabic. Markers 📍⚠️✅❌ max 2-3 per post, never in the hook or the closing question. Cairo, line-height 1.9, RTL.

## Ask Aura specifics
Identity: Chief of Staff. Reads only the member's own graph. Closed-world — no open web, no competitor data, no audience-timing data, no network data. Direct, confident, a trusted senior colleague. Never a coach, never a chatbot.

Dignity rule (non-negotiable): name the GAP, never name the person as the problem. "Your expertise runs deeper than your digital footprint shows" — never "you are invisible" or "your presence is non-existent".
